Quick answer

Octopus Energy is a UK energy supplier known for renewable electricity, digital account tools, smart tariffs and customer service. In April 2026, Octopus said it had passed 8 million UK customers, and Which? named Octopus a Recommended Provider for energy in 2026.

That does not mean Octopus is automatically the cheapest choice for every home. Your best option depends on your postcode, meter type, payment method, electricity and gas usage, whether you have a smart meter, and whether you use an EV, heat pump, solar panels or battery storage.

Common questions

Is Octopus Energy the official supplier behind this website?

No. OctopusReferral.energy is an independent referral website. It links to the official Octopus quote journey, but it is not run by Octopus Energy.

How does the Octopus referral credit work?

Octopus says qualifying home energy referrals give £50 account credit to the referred customer and £50 credit to the referrer. Credit is normally added after the new customer is on supply and the first successful direct debit has been taken.

Can every customer get a referral reward?

No. Existing Octopus customers, customers who have recently been with Octopus, customers supplied under some related brands, and households that already claimed a switching incentive may be excluded. Octopus terms control whether a referral is eligible.

Which tariffs should I compare?

Most households should start by comparing the quote offered at sign-up, then check whether a smart tariff is available after joining. Octopus lists smart tariffs such as Agile, Tracker, Intelligent Octopus Go, Cosy, Flux and Outgoing. Some of those need a smart meter or qualifying technology.

What changed in 2026?

The Ofgem price cap for a typical dual-fuel Direct Debit household fell to £1,641 per year for 1 April to 30 June 2026. That figure is a benchmark, not a personal bill estimate. Your annual cost depends on how much energy you use and the exact rates available in your region.
