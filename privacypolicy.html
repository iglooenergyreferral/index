Who we are

OctopusReferral.energy is an independent referral website. It is not operated by Octopus Energy. Referral buttons link to the official Octopus Energy website.

What information we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect the details you choose to send, such as your name, email address and message. You should not send bank details, meter numbers, Octopus login details, full bills or unnecessary account information.

Why we use contact form information

Contact form information is used to respond to referral questions, help match a qualifying referral where needed, and arrange the extra £25 payment offered on this website after a qualifying switch is confirmed.

Who processes the form

The contact form is handled through Formspree. Formspree processes the message so it can be delivered to this website's owner. You can read Formspree's security and privacy information at formspree.io/security.

Analytics and cookies

This website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and improve the site. Google may set cookies or process usage information according to its own policies. You can read Google's cookie information at policies.google.com/technologies/cookies.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. If there is a referral tracking issue, we may ask you to contact Octopus directly. We only share information with Octopus if it is necessary to resolve a referral query and you have asked us to help with that query.

Retention

Referral messages are kept only for as long as needed to answer the query, arrange the extra payment where applicable, and keep a basic record of payment status.

Your choices

You can ask for your contact form message to be deleted once your referral query has been resolved. You can also avoid the form and contact Octopus Energy directly for account, billing, switching or tariff support.
